Fee Structure

The challenge fee is a single flat payment of $50 — paid once upfront to activate your challenge. There are no deferred payments, no hidden fees, and no additional charges at any stage.

  • TRC20 entry: minimum $48 USDT received to activate
  • BEP20 entry: minimum $49 USDC received to activate
  • The $50 challenge fee is non-refundable under all circumstances

Payment Process

  • Select your preferred network (TRC20 or BEP20) at checkout
  • Send $50 USDT (TRC20) or $50 USDC (BEP20) to the wallet address shown at checkout
  • The system monitors the blockchain automatically for incoming transactions
  • Once the required confirmations are detected and the amount meets the minimum threshold, your challenge is activated automatically
  • You will receive an email notification when your challenge is activated

Irreversibility of Transactions

  • All cryptocurrency transactions are final and irreversible once confirmed on the blockchain
  • We cannot reverse, refund, or recover any payment once it has been sent
  • The $50 challenge fee is non-refundable under all circumstances
  • You are solely responsible for ensuring you send funds to the correct wallet address on the correct network
  • We are not liable for any funds sent to an incorrect address or on the wrong network

Wallet Address Verification

Always verify the wallet address shown at checkout carefully before sending any funds. Our wallet addresses will always be displayed directly on our Platform at fortiqfunded.com. We will never ask you to send funds to a wallet address provided via email, social media, or any other channel.

Payouts and Commissions

All payouts to funded traders and referral commissions are made in USDT (TRC20) or USDC (BEP20), matching the network you used for your original challenge payment. You must provide a valid wallet address on the correct network. We are not responsible for payouts or commissions sent to an incorrect wallet address provided by you.

Payouts and referral commissions are processed every Saturday.
