Important — Read Before Sending Payment

Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible. Once you send funds to our wallet address, the transaction cannot be reversed, recalled, or refunded under any circumstances. Please verify all details carefully before sending.

Payment Process

The payment and activation process works as follows:

  • Select your preferred network (TRC20 or BEP20) at checkout
  • You will be shown the correct wallet address for your chosen network
  • Send $6 USDT (TRC20) or $6 USDC (BEP20) to that address from your own wallet
  • The system monitors the blockchain automatically for incoming transactions
  • Once the required confirmations are detected and the amount meets the minimum threshold, your challenge is activated automatically
  • You will receive an email notification when your challenge is activated
  • If you send less than the minimum required, your challenge will not activate

Fee Structure

The total challenge fee is $150, split into two payments:

  • $6 entry fee today — paid upfront to activate your Stage 1 challenge account
  • $144 balance fee — paid only when you have passed the challenge and request your first payout

The $144 balance is not charged unless you pass the challenge. If you do not pass, you only ever pay the $6 entry fee.

Fee tolerance (to allow for network withdrawal fees):

  • TRC20 entry: we require a minimum of $4.50 USDT received to activate
  • BEP20 entry: we require a minimum of $5.50 USDC received to activate
  • Balance payment (either network): we require a minimum of $142 received to confirm

If your challenge does not activate due to insufficient funds received, you must send the remaining amount or contact our support team at support@fortiqfunded.com.

Irreversibility of Transactions

By making a payment, you fully acknowledge and accept that:

  • All cryptocurrency transactions are final and irreversible once confirmed on the blockchain
  • We cannot reverse, refund, or recover any payment once it has been sent
  • The $6 entry fee is non-refundable under all circumstances, including failure to pass the challenge, change of mind, or technical issues on your end
  • The $144 balance fee is similarly non-refundable once sent
  • You are solely responsible for ensuring you send funds to the correct wallet address on the correct network
  • We are not liable for any funds sent to an incorrect address or on the wrong network

Wallet Address Verification

Always verify the wallet address shown at checkout carefully before sending any funds. Our wallet addresses will always be displayed directly on our Platform. We will never ask you to send funds to a wallet address provided via email, social media, or any channel other than the checkout page of our official Platform at fortiqfunded.com.

If you receive any communication asking you to send funds to an address not shown on our official checkout page, treat it as fraudulent and contact us immediately at support@fortiqfunded.com.

Payouts

All payouts to funded traders are made in USDT (TRC20) or USDC (BEP20), matching the network you used for your original challenge entry payment. You must provide a valid wallet address on the correct network to receive your payout. We are not responsible for payouts sent to an incorrect or incompatible wallet address provided by you.

To request a payout, you must first submit your $144 balance payment via the dashboard. Once confirmed on-chain, your payout is queued for the next Saturday payout cycle.

Risk Warning

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile. Participation in the Fortiq Funded evaluation programme involves the non-refundable payment of a $6 challenge entry fee, and there is no guarantee of passing the evaluation or receiving a payout. The $144 balance is only payable upon passing and requesting your payout.

Only participate with funds you can afford to lose. The challenge entry fee should be treated as a cost of participation, not an investment.
